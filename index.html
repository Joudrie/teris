Material Blocks

How to play

Pieces made of different materials fall from the top. Put materials next to the ones that cancel them out. Whatever cancels drains away and fills the bar beside the board. Fill the bar to clear the level and drop 1 ft down the tower. If the board fills to the top, you lose.

  • Swipe left or right to move
  • Drag down to drop faster
  • Flick down to slam (smashes glass)
  • Tap to rotate
  • Swipe up to use your power when the meter is full

What cancels what

    Powers

    Draining a lot quickly builds a combo and fills your power meter faster. Each level has one power.

      Two-material pieces

      From level 4 some pieces are half one material and half another. Rotate them so each half lands next to what cancels it.
